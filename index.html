About

A little about me

I am an Electrical Engineering student at the University of British Columbia. I really enjoy hardware and electronics, especially projects where I can understand how the different parts of a system interact instead of working on each block in isolation.

Recently, I have also become interested in hardware for machine learning applications. I am currently working on a hardware-focused ML project using PyTorch, where I am learning more about how models are implemented and evaluated while exploring the compute, memory, and hardware constraints that matter when moving ML from software toward real systems.

Outside of engineering, I play piano and I am currently working on several Tchaikovsky pieces, with the goal of bringing my playing to a performance level over the next year. I also enjoy reading biographies and books about history.
