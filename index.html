Analog | Embedded Systems

Smart Temperature Monitoring & Control System

Completed

I built a temperature monitoring and control system around an NTC thermistor, Wheatstone bridge, analog amplification, and Arduino. I fitted a four-coefficient Steinhart-Hart model in Python and used the resulting coefficients in the embedded firmware to calculate temperature from the measured thermistor resistance.

I also built comparator and logic circuitry for different motor-control ranges and used 555 timers for approximately 50% PWM and timed operation.

±0.1°C calibrated accuracy 4-coefficient Steinhart-Hart ~50% PWM 10 Hz logging

Research
